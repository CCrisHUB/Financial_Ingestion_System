2. PDF EXTRACTION REGEX PATTERNS (MCCOY FCU SPECIFIC)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - REGEX_DATE: r"^\d{2}\s?[A-Za-z]{3}\*?"
  - REGEX_AMOUNT: r"-?\$?\d{1,3}(?:,\d{3})*\.\d{2}"
  - REGEX_CHECK: r"(?i)(?:check|draft)\s+(\d{3,5})"
